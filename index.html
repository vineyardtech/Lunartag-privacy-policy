LunarTag ("we", "our", "us", or "the app"), developed and operated by Vineyard Tech, is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how LunarTag collects, processes, stores, and safeguards information when you download, install, and use the LunarTag mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and How We Handle It

  • Camera & Captured Photos (Storage & Gallery): LunarTag uses your device camera strictly to capture workplace and field photos at your request. Captured images are stamped locally on your device with optional company labels, timestamps, GPS coordinates, and street addresses, and are saved to your device storage and local Room database (photos) along with delivery status metadata (PENDING, SCHEDULED, SENT, FAILED, or ARCHIVED). Vineyard Tech does not upload your captured photos to Vineyard Tech servers. Photos are shared outside your device only when you manually share them or schedule them to be sent via your selected messaging application.
  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS & Geocoding): With your permission, LunarTag accesses your device's precise (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) and approximate (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) location using Google Play Services Location and Android Geocoder to stamp live latitude, longitude, and reverse-geocoded street addresses onto your captured photos, embed EXIF location metadata, and compare capture coordinates against administrator-configured local geofence locations (manual_locations). Location data is processed for on-device stamping and verification; we do not continuously track your location in the background for advertising or profiling.
  • Authentication, Cloud Logs & Configuration (Firebase): LunarTag uses Google Firebase Anonymous Authentication to generate an anonymous User ID (uid) for secure access to backend configuration and operational audit logs. Operational event entries (such as photo capture/send status logs, timestamps, event types, anonymous user ID, and basic device/role metadata) may be synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore (audit_logs, users, and tenant configuration documents).
  • Installed Applications List (Target App Selection): LunarTag queries installed applications locally on your device solely so you can select which messaging or sharing app to use as your delivery target. Your list of installed apps stays strictly on your device and is never uploaded, collected, or shared with Vineyard Tech or third parties.
  • Schedules, Presets & Diagnostic Logs (Local Storage): Daily sending time slots, custom company watermark labels, manual geofence locations, and local audit logs are stored locally in your device's private SharedPreferences and Room database (lunartag_database).
  • Support Inquiries: When you contact us through the in-app Contact screen or via email, the information you provide (such as your name, email address, and message) is used solely to respond to your support request or bug report.

2. Device Permissions, Overlay & Accessibility Disclosure

  • Camera, Location, Photos/Media & Notifications: Requested during onboarding and used strictly for capturing geotagged photos, storing/displaying them in your gallery, and posting foreground-service/alarm notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) when scheduled sends execute.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used solely when you enable the floating marker overlay to position on-screen tap coordinates for scheduled sending configuration. Coordinates are saved strictly locally on your device.
  • Accessibility Service (LunarTagAccessibilityService): LunarTag includes an optional Accessibility Service that is disabled by default and operates only if you explicitly enable it in Android Accessibility Settings. When active during a user-scheduled send, it detects the selected target messaging app window and performs automated tap gestures at your configured coordinates to complete scheduled image dispatch. LunarTag does NOT use the Accessibility Service to read, capture, record, or transmit personal messages, screen text, passwords, keystrokes, or sensitive user data to Vineyard Tech or any third party.
  • Exact Alarms & Background Execution: LunarTag uses Android AlarmManager and a foreground service solely to dispatch user-scheduled photos at configured time slots. You can cancel all pending scheduled tasks anytime via the in-app Panic Stop control.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

LunarTag integrates the following Google third-party services, which may automatically collect and process device, diagnostic, and usage information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Displays banner and interstitial advertisements in the app. AdMob may process your Android Advertising ID (AAID), App Set ID, IP address and approximate location (general country/city level), app interactions (launches, taps, ad interactions), and performance diagnostics (launch time, hang rate, energy usage).
  • Google Firebase (Anonymous Auth, Cloud Firestore, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging & Analytics): Used to assign an anonymous user identifier, store operational audit logs and role/tenant configurations, fetch remote app settings, deliver push sync/configuration messages (FCM), and measure anonymized app usage analytics and stability.

For more information on how Google processes data, please review:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Account & Data Deletion (Retention & User Controls)

Vineyard Tech provides both instant on-device controls and email-based deletion requests for LunarTag users, without requiring you to reinstall the app.

How to Request Data & Anonymous Account Record Deletion (Without Reinstalling LunarTag)

  1. Submit a Deletion Request by Email: Send an email to contact@vineyardtech.online or josymvine@gmail.com with the subject line LunarTag Account and Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include Identifying Details: Provide your email address (for support correspondence) and, if applicable, your device/tenant reference or anonymous Firebase UID shown in logs/support tickets so we can locate and purge matching cloud records.

Processing Timeframe: All verified deletion requests for cloud audit/user records and support correspondence are completed within 30 days of receipt, with confirmation sent to your email.

In-App & Device-Level Controls

  • Manage or Delete Local Photos & Logs: Delete captured photo entries directly from the in-app Gallery, clear local logs from the Logs screen, or cancel all scheduled sends from the Dashboard.
  • Revoke Permissions Anytime: Disable Camera, Location, Storage, Notifications, Display Over Other Apps, or the optional Accessibility Service at any time in your Android device Settings.
  • Uninstall App: Uninstalling LunarTag permanently deletes the local Room database (lunartag_database), saved coordinates, schedules, manual geofence locations, and private app preferences. (Note: Photos already exported to your public device gallery remain on your device unless you delete them in your Photos/Gallery app.)

Data Deleted, Data Retained & Retention Periods

  • Local App Data (Deleted Immediately): Local Room database records, robot target coordinates, schedules, manual location presets, and app preferences are erased immediately upon clearing app data or uninstalling LunarTag.
  • Cloud Firestore Records (Users & Audit Logs): Anonymous user/device entries and operational audit logs stored in Cloud Firestore are retained only as long as needed for app operation and workplace auditing, and are permanently deleted within 30 days upon a verified deletion request.
  • Support Inquiries: Emails and support messages sent to Vineyard Tech are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your ticket (up to 90 days if inactive), or deleted within 30 days upon request.
  • Third-Party Google Telemetry (AdMob & Firebase Analytics): Advertising and aggregated analytics telemetry processed by Google is governed by Google's retention policies. You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID anytime via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Security of Your Information

All network communication conducted by LunarTag—including Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Remote Config, FCM, Analytics) and Google AdMob requests—is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Cloud Firestore access is protected by authentication-required security rules. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, location data, or photos to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

LunarTag is an operational and workplace photo-geotagging utility intended for adults and professional users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
